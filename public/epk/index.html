Filmmaker Biography

Short Biography

Zeehad Haque is a Bangladeshi independent filmmaker working across directing, writing, cinematography and editing. Born on July 23, 2004, in Mymensingh, Bangladesh, his work explores psychological tension and atmospheric visual storytelling, with an emphasis on framing, sound, silence and mood.

Extended Biography

Zeehad Haque (born July 23, 2004, in Mymensingh, Bangladesh) is a Bangladeshi independent filmmaker who takes a hands-on, multidisciplinary approach to filmmaking, working as a director, writer, cinematographer and editor. His creative approach emphasizes visual composition, atmosphere, sound design and psychological tension. Rather than relying heavily on dialogue, he is interested in using images, movement, silence and sound to shape the audience's experience. His independent short film Shadow / III (2026) reflects this interest in psychological storytelling and visual silence.

Director's Statement

Independent filmmaking is never about waiting for the ideal budget or a large crew; it is about perspective, patience, and telling an honest story with whatever tools are within reach. In my work, I strip away unnecessary dialogue to let silence, shadow, and camera movement speak. Every frame is crafted to pull the audience into an emotional and psychological space where what is unseen feels just as powerful as what is on screen.

— Zeehad Haque
