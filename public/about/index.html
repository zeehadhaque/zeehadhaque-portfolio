Zeehad Haque (born Khairul Islam Jihad on 23 July, 2004)

I’m an independent filmmaker from Gazipur, Bangladesh. I write, direct, shoot and edit stories shaped by psychological tension, visual silence and atmosphere. I’m drawn to the moments when framing, movement, light and sound can say what dialogue cannot.

01 / The filmmaker

The person behind
the frame.

My filmmaking practice is independent and mobile-first. I work with available tools, natural light and real locations, treating constraints as part of the visual language. Every frame, movement, silence and sound cue should earn its place in the story.

I begin with what a scene should make the audience feel. From there, I shape framing, blocking, movement, available light, edit rhythm and sound until every choice belongs to the story.

04 / Working method

Small setup.
Clear intention.

Every creative decision begins with the story. I shape performance, composition, camera movement, available light, editing, colour and sound around the feeling a scene needs to leave behind. The tools may be modest; the intention behind each frame is not.
